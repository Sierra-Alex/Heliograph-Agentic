# MdProductCarousel

The workhorse product section: an optional `HorizontalHeadline`, then a white caption card and a gradient image panel side by side with an 8px gap inside an 80/48-padded surface panel. Captions are `CarouselStep`s — one active at a time, its rule filling as the slide plays.

Two to four steps. `imagePlacement="left"` mirrors the row; alternate it when two of these sections sit together. The panel gradient follows the page's hue and pairs complementary colours — leave those pairings to the token.
