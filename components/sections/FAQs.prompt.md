# Faqs

The standard FAQ section: a 32px-radius panel on a contrasting page surface, centred H3 title at 150% leading, then a stack of `FaqItem` rows capped at 840px. One row is open at a time — `defaultOpen` sets which, `-1` opens none.

Pick `pageColor` from the page's hue and set `onDark` when the surface is one of the deep hues; the panel then becomes a bordered transparent shell instead of a lighter fill.
