# ResourceCardLockup

The resources row: an optional `HorizontalCoreType` header, then one to six cards in a 16px-gapped row. `1-lg` is a single split card; the mixed options put the large card first and small cards beside it; `4-sm` and `6-sm` wrap to a three-column grid.

`pageBackground` sets the page surface and picks the card fill with it — light pages take grey cards, tinted pages take cards in the same hue.
