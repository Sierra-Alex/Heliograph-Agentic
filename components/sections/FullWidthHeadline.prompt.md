# FullWidthHeadline

A single long statement running the width of the page — three or four sentences of H3, no tag, no button, no image. Used between product sections as a change of pace. On a deep hue surface keep `darkTheme` and match `color` to the page's hue; on a light surface set `darkTheme={false}` and the headline takes the standard on-light colour.

One per page at most. It is a pause, not a section header.
