# LgProductCarousel

The largest product section: a `FullWidthHeadline` across the top, one 1:2 image panel beneath it, and a row of captions that act as the carousel's tabs — the selected one at full opacity, the rest at 60%.

Three captions is the source layout; four still fits. On `backgroundColor="dark"` the surface takes the hue's deep step and the headline takes its tinted colour. Light and muted keep the on-light text colours.
