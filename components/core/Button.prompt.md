Pill button with a trailing arrow that extends on hover — use it for every call to action on marketing surfaces.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Get Started</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="lg">Learn More</Button>
```

- `variant`: primary (solid near-black on light / white on dark), secondary (7% tint), tertiary (transparent, no fill).
- `size`: lg (default) or sm — sm is what the top nav uses.
- `onDark`: flips the fill/text pairing for dark banners and footers.
- Labels are Title Case unless longer than five words. Arrow is always present; do not add your own icon.
