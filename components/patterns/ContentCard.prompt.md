# ContentCard

The blog / editorial card: an `ImageRatio` well inside an 8px-padded 24px-radius card, then H5 headline at 140%, B4 subhead, and a tertiary link. `bigImage` gives the 1:2 banner crop used in two-up rows; turn it off for the 1:1 square used in three- and four-up grids.

Card fill is `--surface-default`; on a white page that reads as a soft panel, so put these on white or muted surfaces, not on `--surface-default` itself.
