# FormLockup

The form card used on demo, contact and newsletter pages: a white 24px-radius card with a 32px pad, an 8px white halo and a soft purple glow behind it. Fields stack 8px apart, paired fields sit 24px apart in a row, and the submit button hangs 40px below the field set.

`formType` picks the field set — Contact Sales is the four-row version, Get Demo drops the message, Newsletter is email only. Pass extra fields as children; they land after the standard set.
